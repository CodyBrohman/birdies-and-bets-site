Points between friends. No real money, ever.

Built for the course

A proper scorecard first. Everything else on top.

Tap a score chip one hole at a time and the hole advances itself. Front nine, back nine, birdies and bogeys marked, handicap strokes shown on every hole.

Works without a signal

Search thousands of courses and download the scorecard, or add one by hand in a minute. Once the course is loaded the whole round works in airplane mode, every score is saved instantly, and the screen stays awake.

Handicaps done properly

Enter each player's index and the app computes the course handicap for the tee you're playing, shows exactly which holes get a stroke, and lets each game score gross or net. Plus handicaps, allowances, back nine and shotgun starts are all handled.

Keep the round, share the card

Every finished round is kept: the full card, the game results and who owes whom. Share the scorecard as an image to the group chat, and back everything up to a file for a new phone.

Game formats

The games you already play, scored for you.

Pick as many as the group wants before you tee off, or add one mid-round. Each recalculates from the full card, so a fixed score three holes back fixes the standings too.

What it doesn't do

A tally, and nothing more.

Birdies & Bets keeps a tally of points between players. It has no real-money gambling, no deposits, no withdrawals, no payments, and no links to payment services. Settling up is between friends, in the parking lot, as it always was.

  • No account and no sign-in
  • Nothing you enter leaves your phone unless you share or back it up
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking
  • Stakes are points. The app never touches money
