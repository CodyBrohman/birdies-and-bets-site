One hole at a time.

Score the hole, and the games and the card take care of themselves. Standings update before you reach the next tee.

  1. 01

    Score the hole

    Tap for par, minus for a birdie, plus for a bogey. Handicap strokes show on the holes that get them.

  2. 02

    The games keep up

    Skins carry, the hot seat changes hands, the match swings. Every game recalculates the moment the hole is scored.

  3. 03

    The card keeps itself

    Birdies circled, bogeys boxed, gross or net with one tap. Share it to the group chat at the 19th.

    Straight talk

    A tally, and nothing more.

    Birdies & Bets keeps a tally of points between players. No real-money gambling, no deposits, no withdrawals, no payouts, no prizes, no links to payment services. Points are a tally between players, and nothing is paid between them in the app.

    • No account needed. Sign in with Apple or an email code to sync your rounds between phones
    • Crash reports with no names, scores or courses. On unless you turn them off in Settings
    • Usage stats only if you opt in. No advertising, and no tracking across other apps or websites
    • Stakes are points. The app never touches money
