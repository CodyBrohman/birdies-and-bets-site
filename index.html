Handicaps done properly

Enter each index and the app works out the course handicap for the tee you're on, marks the holes that get a stroke, and lets every game score gross or net. Plus handicaps, allowances, back nine and shotgun starts included.

Works without a signal

Once the course is loaded, the whole round runs in airplane mode.

Share the card

Saved on every tap

Thousands of courses

Straight talk

A tally, and nothing more.

Birdies & Bets keeps a tally of points between players. No real-money gambling, no deposits, no withdrawals, no payments, no links to payment services. Settling up is between friends, in the parking lot, as it always was.

  • No account and no sign-in
  • Nothing leaves your phone unless you share or back it up
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking
  • Stakes are points. The app never touches money
