One hole at a time.

Score the hole, and the games and the card take care of themselves. Standings update before you reach the next tee.

    Score the hole

    Skins carry, the hot seat changes hands, presses kick in. Every game recalculates the moment the hole is scored.

  3. 03

    Birdies circled, bogeys boxed, gross or net with one tap. Share it to the group chat at the 19th.

    Handicaps done properly

    Enter each index and the app works out the course handicap for the tee you're on, marks the holes that get a stroke, and lets every game score gross or net. Plus handicaps, allowances, back nine and shotgun starts included.

    Works without a signal

    Once the course is loaded, the whole round runs in airplane mode.

    Share the card

    The finished scorecard as an image, straight to the group chat.

    Saved on every tap

    Close the app mid-round, reopen it, and Home offers to resume where you left off.

    Thousands of courses

    Search and download the scorecard, or add a course by hand in a minute. Backups to a file for a new phone.

    Straight talk

    A tally, and nothing more.

    Birdies & Bets keeps a tally of points between players. No real-money gambling, no deposits, no withdrawals, no payments, no links to payment services. Settling up is between friends, in the parking lot, as it always was.

    • Sign in with Apple or an email code, and your rounds sync to your account
    • Anonymous crash reports, which you can turn off in Settings
    • Usage stats only if you opt in. No advertising, no tracking
    • Stakes are points. The app never touches money
