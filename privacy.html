Privacy Policy

Birdies & Bets: Golf Scorecard · Effective 7 October 2026

Birdies & Bets is a golf scorecard app that keeps score and tracks the side games a group agrees to play. You sign in so your rounds follow you to any phone. It keeps working offline on the course and catches up when it has signal.

What we collect

Only what the app needs to keep your golf with you. There is no advertising, we never sell data, and the app does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

  • Your account. When you sign in with Apple we receive a private account identifier from Apple, and your name and email only if you choose to share them (Apple lets you hide your email behind a relay address). When you sign in by email we receive your email address, used only to send your sign-in codes.
  • Your name as you want it shown on scorecards.
  • Your golf. The rounds you play and score, your round history, the players you add (their names and handicap indexes, as you enter them), courses you create or import, and the round settings and preferences that should follow you between phones (default stake label, handicap allowance, your bag and home area).

We use this only to run the app: to sign you in, to keep your data in sync between your phones, and to bring it back if you reinstall or change phones. Device-only settings (haptics, your privacy choices, your profile photo) stay on the phone.

Two more things can leave your phone, and neither is linked to your account: anonymous crash reports, which you can turn off, and anonymous usage counts, which are off unless you turn them on. Both are under Profile → Settings → Privacy.

Where it is kept

Your account and synced data are stored with Supabase (operated by Supabase, Inc.), our database provider, protected so that only your signed-in account can read or change it. A copy also stays in the app's private storage on your phone so the app works without signal. Signing out removes that copy from the phone; your account keeps it.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the app under Profile → Settings → Account → Delete account. That permanently deletes your account and everything stored with it from our database, and clears it from the phone. You can also email us to ask for deletion. If you only want to stop using one phone, sign out instead.

Shared scorecards

When you share a finished scorecard, the app sends the picture of the card along with a link to it. The link itself carries what is on that card: the course and tee names, the date, the hole pars, each player's name, scores and handicap strokes, and the game results. That information sits in the part of the link after the #, which web browsers never send to a server, so our website never receives it. The page at www.birdiesandbets.com/card reads it inside the recipient's browser, or the app opens it directly. Nothing from a shared card is stored by us. Anyone who has the link can see the card, so share it with the people you played with.

Network requests the app makes

  • Update checks. On launch the app may ask Expo's update service (EAS Update, operated by Expo / 650 Industries) whether a newer version of the app's code is available. That request carries the app version, platform and runtime identifier, and Expo's servers see your IP address. It carries no scores, names or courses.
  • Course search. When you type in the course search box, the text you type is sent to our course lookup service (a Cloudflare Worker we operate) which forwards it to GolfCourseAPI to find scorecards. Those services see the search text and your IP address. Nothing else from the app is sent. Courses you download are stored on your phone; saved courses and manual entry work without any connection.

Crash reports

When the app hits an error, it can send a crash report to Sentry (operated by Functional Software, Inc.) so we can find and fix the bug. Crash reports are on by default, and you can turn them off at any time under Settings → Privacy → Crash reports. Nothing is sent before your settings have loaded.

A crash report contains the error message and where in the app's code it happened, the screens you moved through just before it, your phone model, iOS version, app version and update ID. Before a report leaves your phone the app removes your device's name, and it never includes player names, scores, courses, notes or anything you typed. The report carries no account, advertising ID or other identifier for you. Sentry's servers see your IP address when the report arrives; we do not store it with the report. Reports are deleted automatically within 90 days.

Usage analytics

If you say yes, the app sends anonymous usage counts to Aptabase, a privacy-first analytics service, so we can see which features get used. Usage analytics are off unless you turn them on, either when the app first asks or under Settings → Privacy → Usage analytics, and you can turn them off at any time.

The only events are: a round started (how many holes and players, which games, shotgun start or not), a round finished or discarded (how many holes were scored), a course imported or created, a scorecard shared, and a backup saved or restored. Each event also carries your iOS version, app version, language setting and a random session number that changes after an hour without use. There are no names, scores, course names, locations, device identifiers or advertising IDs. Aptabase does not use cookies or fingerprinting and does not store IP addresses.

Money

The app does not hold, move, or request money. It shows a tally of points between players and provides no way to pay, deposit, withdraw, or transfer anything. It contains no links to payment services.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will delete it. Crash reports and usage counts are anonymous and contain nothing that identifies the person using the app.
