Privacy Policy

Birdies & Bets: Golf Scorecard · Effective 22 September 2026

Birdies & Bets is a golf scorecard app that keeps score and tracks the side games a group agrees to play. It is built to work on your phone, offline, without an account.

What we collect

Nothing about you or your golf. The app has no user accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no crash reporting service. Scores, player names, handicap indexes and course details are never sent to us.

What stays on your device

The scores, player names, handicap indexes, courses and round history you enter are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. Deleting the app deletes them. They leave your phone only when you choose to share a scorecard image or save a backup file, and then only to the app or place you pick.

Network requests the app makes

  • Update checks. On launch the app may ask Expo's update service (EAS Update, operated by Expo / 650 Industries) whether a newer version of the app's code is available. That request carries the app version, platform and runtime identifier, and Expo's servers see your IP address. It carries no scores, names or courses.
  • Course search. When you type in the course search box, the text you type is sent to our course lookup service (a Cloudflare Worker we operate) which forwards it to GolfCourseAPI to find scorecards. Those services see the search text and your IP address. Nothing else from the app is sent. Courses you download are stored on your phone; saved courses and manual entry work without any connection.

Money

The app does not hold, move, or request money. It shows a tally of points between players and provides no way to pay, deposit, withdraw, or transfer anything. It contains no links to payment services.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
